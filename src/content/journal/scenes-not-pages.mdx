This site is told as a sequence of scenes. Each project gets one pinned section, driven by scroll.

## Three rules

1. Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.
2. One protagonist animation per scene.
3. Scrub follows the scroll, never the clock.

## When to stop

On small screens and with reduced motion, the scenes become plain stacked sections. The content works without any JavaScript at all.
